# Synthesis outline

Use locally or for authorized read-only synthesis. Supply the original question, actual decision/source anchors, findings and consequential limitations. Do not assume multiple investigators or all evidence categories were searched.

Follow [epistemics.md](epistemics.md). Distinguish direct evidence, supported conclusions, inference, speculation and unknowns. Cite actual sources for material claims. Code proves mechanics, not its own motivation. Verify citations when a claim is consequential or uncertain.

Read all supplied findings, deduplicate overlapping evidence and surface contradictions. Explain the inference step rather than making a supported-looking story from a thin record. Check the user's hypothesis independently.

Lead with the answer and its support. Include competing hypotheses, gaps, searched sources and overall confidence only as needed for the question. A simple cited rationale can be brief. Do not create empty categories, assume git/gh access, or require a seven-source coverage map.

For a prospective change, preserve the established constraints and unresolved risks in the owning Plan. Lasting rationale follows ADR rules; current implementation facts follow Design rules. Apply technical-writing and unslop without stripping evidence-based uncertainty.
